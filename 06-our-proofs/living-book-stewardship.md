# Keeping the Living Book alive: PATAI stewardship

**6 November 2026 — editorial stewardship arrangement accepted by Paul Ransfield.**

The Manifesting Īuwe Living Book records a developing practice, not a finished system. Its purpose is to make the architecture understandable while keeping its history available. The public operating manual, the hau.whanau.tv progress archive and this repository contribute different parts of that account.[1] As local practice changes, the book needs to keep breathing: a reader should be able to see what has moved forward, what has been reconsidered and what remains unfinished.

## A distinct editorial responsibility

The agreed arrangement makes **PATAI the kaitiaki working lane for the Living Book's editorial pathway**. PATAI helps Paul Ransfield identify narrative gaps, prepare additions, check their sources and explain changes in language that future readers can follow. It prepares and maintains the record; it does not become its owner.

Paul remains the repository owner, accountable public editor and final publication authority. AI assistance does not confer community, cultural or legal authority. That distinction already informs the book's invitation to use AI as a patient reading assistant rather than a substitute for community judgement.[2]

IPU retains its separate responsibility for considering evidence packages and admission to its governed records. An editorial update to this book is not an IPU admission. Routine documentation does not have to wait for database intake, table changes or the acceptance of a production package merely to describe the work truthfully. If a page reports an IPU decision, however, it must accurately distinguish an accepted decision from a proposal or an unresolved question.

WAI holds detailed production and review evidence. PUNA supports source discovery; IPAPAWAI supports the design workbench. Their respective threads can review pages that describe their work. This review helps the book remain accurate without making every workstream the gatekeeper for every sentence.

## Change with intergenerational memory

Preserve → Verify → Test → Change → Release is the editorial pathway, with effort proportionate to the change. Preserve the existing history and identify the source or reason for a revision. Verify whether the text describes an observed result, a participant report, an interpretation or a proposal. Test readability, links and the proposed public scope. Then prepare a bounded change for Paul's acceptance and an explicitly authorised release.

For a small correction, a clear diff and meaningful commit message may carry the necessary memory. A substantial architectural change needs a dated explanation and appropriate workstream review. The aim is not more paperwork: it is to avoid losing the reason for a change while allowing useful updates to move at the pace of practice.

Earlier accounts should not disappear merely because the project has progressed. Where a later account supersedes an earlier one, explain what changed and why. Corrections should remain possible without pretending that the earlier uncertainty never existed. This extends the book's existing principle that proof is the minimum truthful record needed to honour a promise, correct an error and learn what should happen next.[3]

## Public book, local workbenches

The Living Book is deliberately published. Local workbench Git repositories preserve runners, recipes, tests and supporting history, but are not automatically published to GitHub. A local commit does not itself mean public release.

The public book can describe source inventories, captioning, Card JSON and provenance practices without exposing private collections, credentials or personal machine paths. It should link suitable public evidence and state when detailed evidence is retained locally. A useful caption remains discovery evidence; its appearance in the book does not establish rights clearance, a selected meaning, a translation or an approved learning event.

This stewardship note does not select repository-wide terms of reuse or settle the separate i-hau and reciprocity discussion. Those require their own consideration. Its immediate purpose is simpler: give the Living Book a clear editorial home, keep it connected to practice and leave an understandable account for the people who come next.
