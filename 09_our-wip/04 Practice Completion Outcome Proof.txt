The learner swipes "learnt" in the Flutter app; this completion event is sent to Strapi (SPPS). When the enabler requests outcome proof, the system queries Strapi for completion counts. If thresholds are met, the enabler receives the outcome bonus — linking payment to actual learning, not just broadcast.
