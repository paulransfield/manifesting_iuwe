Narrative:
A source asset (e.g., an OpenMoji) can have multiple derivatives: gender variants, skin‑tone variants, resized thumbnails, etc. Each derivative is stored as a separate content_assets row with a parent_uuid and derivative_type. The MoW tracks the entire chain, providing immutable provenance.
