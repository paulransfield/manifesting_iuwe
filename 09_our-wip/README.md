# 09. Our WIP

> **Work in progress is where a living system shows its joints. It is not an embarrassment to hide; it is an invitation to test carefully.**

## Purpose of this folder

This folder keeps early schematics, working notes, and provisional architecture visible while they are still becoming. It preserves the trail from an idea through a diagram to a tested implementation, without presenting every early expression as final policy or finished code.

The files here are useful because they show the intended relationship among shared content, local sovereignty, koha, delivery evidence, practice completion, provenance, and the complete ī-puāwai assemblage.

## How to read a WIP artefact

A working note may contain a useful pattern, a prior term, an untested assumption, or a technology choice that has since changed. For example, earlier diagrams use language that has now been refined: **koha** replaces earlier terminology, clean UUID identity replaces filename identity, and ī-puāwai makes word senses and provenance relationships more explicit.

A WIP artefact should therefore be read as evidence of the design journey. When an idea becomes durable, its current expression belongs in the numbered chapters or the ī-puāwai section, with an appropriate link back to its earlier source.

## The discipline of becoming

| When a working idea changes | Record the change by |
|---|---|
| A term is refined | Updating the canonical chapter and noting the earlier usage. |
| A database relationship becomes real | Linking the schema or implementation note in 08-our-i-puawai. |
| A technology changes | Recording why the former option was insufficient or superseded. |
| A community question is unresolved | Leaving it visible as a question rather than pretending consensus exists. |
| A test fails or reveals a boundary | Keeping the result as a learning record for the next iteration. |
