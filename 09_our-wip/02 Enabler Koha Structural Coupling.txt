Narrative:
Enablers pay a koha (e.g., $30) for a delivery event. 80% flows back to the community trust that supports local curation and learner engagement; 20% sustains the core federation operations. This funds legal personhood and the ongoing structural coupling that keeps the ecosystem viable. A service is provided where the accountant gets clean, traceable numbers named koha. The community gets koha. The PracticeEvent table stays pure.
