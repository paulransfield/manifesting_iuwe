# 04. Our Pathways

> **A pathway is how a prepared learning event meets a person in their own place and time.**

## Delivery is part of the pedagogy

A good learning event can still fail if it arrives at the wrong time, with the wrong language, without enough context, or without a reliable way for a learner to return. Our Pathways holds the practical architecture of scheduling and delivery: timezones, community rhythms, channels, retries, receipts, and the small decisions that make a sequence feel dependable rather than accidental.

The system is designed for both scheduled one-minute events and longer curated streams. Each community can choose when and how its audience is invited to practise.

## The scheduling record

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Practice event** | The approved text, audio, image, video, or interactive composition. |
| **Community** | The local context responsible for the delivery choice. |
| **Scheduled time** | A timezone-aware local date and time, never an ambiguous server timestamp. |
| **Channel** | Stream, web, app, SMS invitation, email, community post, or another agreed pathway. |
| **Audience context** | Age, cohort, language, campaign, or learner group where appropriate. |
| **Delivery state** | Planned, attempted, served, paused, failed, retired, or re-scheduled. |
| **Evidence link** | The records that later show what occurred. |

## Shared assets, local rhythm

The same approved asset may be scheduled differently in different communities. A Māori whānau, a Pacific church group, a migrant-health cohort, and a future Sami community do not need to share a timetable merely because they share an underlying tool or source asset.

```text
one approved learning event
        ↓
local community chooses language, audience, channel, timezone, and schedule
        ↓
local delivery creates its own evidence and review trail
```

This is a practical expression of sovereignty. Sharing an asset does not require synchronising a community’s daily life to an external platform’s preferred engagement window.

## The 90-second boundary

A learning event may be composed of short elements, including 15-second practice segments, but the intended maximum duration for an individual IUWE learning event is 90 seconds. This preserves a clear unit for scheduling, attention, repetition, and evidence. Longer streams can be made from a sequence of individually addressable events rather than one untraceable file.

## Channels are invitations, not ownership claims

SMS, email, streams, apps, and community discussion are delivery routes. They should not become the sole place where the relationship with a learner lives. Each invitation should lead back toward community-controlled spaces, understandable privacy settings, and a respectful option to stop or change the relationship.

| Channel | Useful role | Must remain true |
|---|---|---|
| **Streaming** | Scheduled and ambient access to short learning sequences | Every item remains identifiable and reviewable. |
| **Mobile app** | Personal practice, progress, and return visits | Learner records need consent and clear purpose. |
| **SMS / email** | Gentle invitations, reminders, and receipt-oriented communication | Consent and opt-out are explicit. |
| **Community discussion** | Non-linear support, questions, correction, and renewal | A word, phrase, or lesson can have a community endpoint. |
