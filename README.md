## A living book, not a finished prospectus

This repository records the **proof of architecture** for community-operated social learning. It is written in public so that a whānau, language community, school, creator collective, or future implementation team can see both the aspiration and the working parts.

The proposition is simple: shared learning assets can travel through many communities without erasing local authority. The commons holds reusable capability; each community retains its own language, schedules, kaitiaki, learner relationships, and decisions about what is appropriate to teach.

> **One garden, many gardeners: shared capability, sovereign local practice.**

## The system in one movement

```text
community ask → seed bank → controlled language sense → approved visual / audio asset
→ practice event → local schedule → delivery evidence → learner completion
→ receipt, review, renewal, and further community work
```

The movement is deliberately circular. A learning asset is not finished when it is published. It remains connected to its source, its conditions of use, its sense, its community review, and the evidence of its delivery.

## Current proof of architecture

The repository now has a practical centre of gravity in **ī-puāwai**: a local language-bank proof built with Flask, SQLite, Jinja, and Fomantic CSS. It carries clean internal identity, word senses, source provenance, controlled media relationships, and pathways to translation, voice, practice, delivery, and community review.

The work is intentionally iterative. A schema, an image test, a community question, or a new language can improve the design. The repository should therefore show decisions clearly, retain their provenance, and leave room for future communities to adapt the pattern without inheriting unexamined assumptions.

## Reading note

Terms including *whānau*, *kaitiaki*, *koha*, *ūkaipō*, and *whakapapa* are used here as part of the project’s lived operating vocabulary. They are not decorative labels. Their local meaning, authority, and use remain matters for the communities who carry them.
