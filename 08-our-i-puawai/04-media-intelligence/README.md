# 04 — media intelligence

**Status: Wonder captioning operational; Papawai truthful-sense-selection test under way.**

Media intelligence is the controlled use of local vision-language models and image-processing tools to make an existing visual collection searchable, describable, and teachable. It does not replace curation or community authority. It accelerates first drafts, surface inconsistencies, and preserves raw model output for review.

This folder separates two related workflows:

| Workflow | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Wonder captioning | Produces rich Adult, Child, Grandparent, OET, and keyword descriptions for visually expressive media. |
| Papawai foundation captioning | Selects truthful controlled senses for simple, curated learning visuals and produces compact 144-character cohort statements. |

Filename wording is supporting context, not truth. OpenMoji, Noun Project, and bilingual curated filenames can help Eagle distinguish simple graphics, but `Filename_Alignment` records whether a hint agrees with the image. The image remains the visual authority.

The downstream [From Curated Media to Federated Learning Event](../06-practice-events-and-delivery/from-curated-media-to-federated-learning-event.md) paper describes how a selected, reviewable visual candidate may later enter a controlled event-preparation path. It does not promote a filename, caption, image discovery result, or category queue into a confirmed word sense, source-rights conclusion, curriculum decision, or publication approval.
