# Whata 14.4k - Edition 1

**Status: a 14,400-title, source-order public learning-set edition. It is a bounded successor to a preserved 14,401-title source order, following one duplicate-occurrence normalisation and one community-governed Edition 1 boundary decision.**

`Whata` names an elevated store for food and other purposes. It can also mean to elevate or support, and to bring into prominence or publish. Here it names a carefully held learning resource: not a closed canon, but a managed public set whose future changes remain visible and community-governed.

This name is offered with a concise acknowledgement of Anituatua Black, whose oral sharing informed the seed-storehouse resonance of `whata`, and of Whatarangi Winiata and Te Wananga o Raukawa. A fuller whakapapa remains oral.

The original source evidence remains preserved. Raw title casing and spelling are retained in this Edition 1 list. One title is deferred from the general-purpose Edition 1 set under decision record `WHT-D-001`; this is not a judgement on the title's meaning or value. Future membership changes require a recorded community decision. Any increase beyond the 14,400 boundary requires an explicit boundary decision.
