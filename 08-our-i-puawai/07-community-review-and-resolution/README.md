A community-held language bank must make correction possible. A source, phoneme, word sense, translation, audio model, visual treatment, or practice event can be questioned, revised, approved, paused, or retired without losing the history that explains why it entered the system.

Discourse provides the non-linear community endpoint: a phoneme, word, phrase, lesson, source, or change can have a threaded discussion record. ī-puāwai holds the structured identity and lifecycle state; Discourse holds the conversation, review, operational change, incident, problem, release, and future API-integration context.

## Rights and reciprocity

Existing licence or permission remains the primary legal basis for use. A Memorandum of Wishes, source rate card, koha/reciprocity commitment, or resolution case does not create an invented retroactive debt. It records a good-faith path to contact, clarify, pause, correct attribution, negotiate where appropriate, or retire an asset before disagreement becomes a dispute.

> **No automatic payment, no invisible use, no invented debt, and no licence bypass. But also no pretending that future value created from a source has no human relationship worth recording.**
