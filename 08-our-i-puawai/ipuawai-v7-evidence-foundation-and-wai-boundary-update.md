# Ī-puāwai v7 and WAI workstream update

**Status: Ī-puāwai has an accepted schema-v7 evidence-registry foundation. The v7 milestone created provenance structure only. Its two new tables—`evidence_packages` and `evidence_record_refs`—remain intentionally empty: no evidence package and no record reference has been admitted.**

## What v7 establishes

Schema v7 provides a small, inspectable container for future evidence work.

| Table | Intended role | Current state |
|---|---|---|
| `evidence_packages` | Holds the bounded identity, purpose, provenance, scope, and stated limits of an evidence package. | Empty. |
| `evidence_record_refs` | Holds declared references between an admitted evidence package and an existing Ī-puāwai record, where such a relationship has separately earned admission. | Empty. |

The v7 milestone followed protected-backup, duplicate-rehearsal, direct-migration governance, read-only verification, and retrospective acceptance. It does **not** constitute evidence intake, semantic selection, translation approval, phrase or media association, curriculum decision, or public release of private evidence.

## Why the tables are empty

The value of an evidence registry is not the existence of table names or technical capacity. It depends on **community-first rules** that make future use accountable: what may be proposed; whose authority is needed; what provenance, permission, and review context must be declared; what a record may and may not claim; how a decision is accepted, deferred, revised, or withdrawn; and how that change remains visible over time.

Until those rules and a bounded use case are agreed, an empty table is the honest state. It prevents a source occurrence, public word list, image, caption, media asset, or available file from being mistaken for an accepted Ī-puāwai relationship.

Another AI-enabled team could reproduce a schema, but a schema alone does not carry community authority, source relationships, decision memory, review practice, or permission boundaries. The Collective Advantage lies in the rules, the attributable decisions, and the ability to change without losing memory—not in a database structure by itself.

## Current boundary with Whata and WAI

[Whata 14.4k — Edition 1](02-language-bank-and-phonemes/01-foundation-word-lists/whata-14-4k/) is a public, community-governed learning corpus. It is an available public reference, not automatic Ī-puāwai title intake, canonical-word selection, meaning authority, translation resource, curriculum sequence, or evidence-registry admission.

The WAI workstream remains independent and production-owned. Its Puna/Aho work may prepare candidate evidence, captions, and media context, but nothing is automatically admitted to Ī-puāwai. Any future convergence must arrive as a bounded external package with declared source, rights, and review context, then pass a separately governed Ī-puāwai intake decision.

## September direction

The next IPU work is to develop the community-first rules and a small, authority-safe use-case design before any evidence-registry record is created. The initial question is not “what can be loaded?” It is “what future relationship is useful, truthful, appropriately authorised, and bounded enough to earn admission?”
