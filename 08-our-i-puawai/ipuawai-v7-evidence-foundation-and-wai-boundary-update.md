# I-puawai v7 and WAI workstream update

**Status: I-puawai has an accepted schema-v7 evidence-registry foundation. The v7 milestone created provenance structure only; no evidence packages or record references have been admitted.**

The v7 milestone followed protected-backup, duplicate-rehearsal, direct-migration governance, read-only verification, and retrospective acceptance. It does not constitute evidence intake, semantic selection, translation approval, phrase or media association, or public release of private evidence.

The WAI workstream remains independent and production-owned. Its Puna/Aho work may prepare candidate evidence, captions, and media context, but nothing is automatically admitted to I-puawai. Any future convergence must arrive as a bounded external package with declared source, rights, and review context, then pass a separately governed IPU intake decision.

> Structure can be ready before content is admitted. A candidate is not an acceptance.
