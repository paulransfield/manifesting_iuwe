# 06 — practice events and delivery

**Status: schema and delivery model designed; event-provenance and federated-media workflow papers published as discussion-and-operating-design material; video, audio, and scheduling tests continuing.**

A practice event is the unit that turns a language-bank relationship into a learnable encounter. It may combine a visual anchor, a short written statement, an acoustic model, a call-and-response prompt, a cohort register, and an intended duration. A practice event can then be scheduled as a one-minute card, included in a longer stream, or delivered through a community-specific pathway.

The delivery lifecycle records:

```text
community ask → scope → recipe → production → scheduled delivery
    → evidence → receipt → review and revision
```

TouchDesigner, livestreaming, scheduled 1-minute media, and federated delivery are complementary entry points. The database does not dictate one presentation platform; it preserves the relationships required to know what was made, why, for whom, when it was delivered, and what evidence or feedback returned.

## Event provenance and federated workflow papers

The [Ī-puāwai Learning-Event Provenance JSON — Design Memorandum](learning-event-provenance-json-design-memorandum.md) proposes a narrow, immutable event-level projection for one declared event revision at one export time. The companion [From Curated Media to Federated Learning Event](from-curated-media-to-federated-learning-event.md) paper describes the controlled pathway from source parent and derivative care through visual discovery, language and Kaitiaki review, voice routes, deterministic assembly, and a community-specific publication context.

Both papers are **discussion-and-operating-design** material. They do not implement an exporter, migrate or ingest a database, approve a local word sense, establish source rights, authorise an event for publication, dispatch an SMS, create a payment or receipt record, or claim learner participation or learning. They make the required relationships and future decision gates legible before any later implementation contract is approved.
