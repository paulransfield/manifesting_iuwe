# The Federated Learning Ecosystems

> **Twelve public learning ecosystems can share a common operating pattern without becoming one undifferentiated platform.**

The federation is not a claim that every community must use the same curriculum, language, schedule, or cultural framing. It is a public, reusable delivery pattern: a community-facing `iho.` learning stream, a local direction of travel, shared technical capability, and the right for each community to determine what enters its own garden.

The twelve public endpoints below are live entry points to the federated streaming pattern. Several currently expose the same early 24/7 teaching-to-learn / learning-to-teach shell or an initializing playlist. That visible commonality is deliberate infrastructure, not a claim that every community-specific stream is already fully curated. Each node is a place where its community pathway can be developed and governed.

## Two complementary delivery surfaces

The same learning ecosystem can meet a learner through two deliberately different surfaces.

| Surface | Public entry point | Basic description |
|---|---|---|
| **24/7 visual stream** | The twelve `iho.` endpoints above | A scheduled, ambient visual/nano-learning stream. It carries the rich media and voice pathway for learners who can access video. |
| **Follow-the-sun low-bandwidth alternative** | [iuwe.bestpractice.tv](https://iuwe.bestpractice.tv/) | A rotating-cube, text-first lesson surface. Its sets update on a three-minute rhythm and expose a short word sequence, a unique SHA-256 identifier, a linked learning locker, and practice-app routes. It is the low-bandwidth scheduled alternative to the full visual stream. |

## How the services fit together

```text
federated iho stream or rotating-cube lesson
                  ↓
community discussion + learning archive
                  ↓
learner practice app / game / self-tracking route
                  ↓
community care key + koha relationship
                  ↓
review, evidence, renewal, and local community capability
```

This is not a single platform trying to own every learner relationship. It is a **federated service constellation**: shared technical capability, multiple public learning doors, local community direction, and traceable care relationships.
